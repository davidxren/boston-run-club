## 3. The inspiration and the actual wedge

Inspiration is **Cluby**, an NYC app in private beta as of Sept 2026, being built by @lindsiannshi. Her pitch, from her Instagram stories: there is no platform to discover communities and clubs, when she moved to NYC she had to watch TikToks of people ranking run clubs, and existing community apps feel outdated and not built for Gen Z. Cluby covers run clubs, book clubs, supper clubs and niche communities. She is recruiting first users through a Google Form called "Cluby First Users" that asks about how long you have lived in NYC, how you currently find clubs (Instagram, TikTok, Partiful, Eventbrite/Meetup, Volo, Reddit, Google, friends), what problems you have finding community, and whether you would test it and hop on a feedback call.

I am building the Boston version and I intend to beat it.

**The thesis, and it is the whole product:** friendships come from showing up to the same thing repeatedly with the same people. They do not come from matching. Friend-matching apps feel like blind dates with extra steps, and the awkward matching flow is not where 90% of real friendships come from.

So the product does exactly two things:

1. **Removes first-visit uncertainty.** Where do I stand, what actually happens, how fast is it, will I get dropped, who shows up, what happens after, does it cost anything. No existing product answers this per club.
2. **Makes repeat attendance visible.** You have been 3 times. You have run with these 4 people twice or more.

**Hard bans, do not propose these:** no swiping, no 1:1 matching, no DMs, no friend requests, no follows, no "people you may like," no star ratings, no streaks or badges or points or leaderboards.

## 6. Product decisions, locked

- The unit is the **session**, not the person.
- Occurrences (a session on a concrete date) are computed for the next 14 days, never stored.
- "I'm going" with a first-timer flag. Shows "6 going, 2 first-timers." Never shows names.
- "I went" check-in, available from start time to 36 hours after.
- Faces list: after 2+ shared check-ins at the same club, and only if both people opted in, you see a display name and "ran together 3 times." Nothing tappable. Section heading is the exact string "Ran with." Default for the opt-in is off.
- Missing data renders as missing with an exact string, never inferred. Every empty field links to a suggest-an-edit form.
- Meet spots render as circles sized by precision. A pin only when precision is exact.
- Every seeded club ships `verified=false` and displays "Unverified. Last checked Sep 2, 2026" until a human checks the Instagram.
- Vibe tags with vote counts, not star ratings. Voting requires a check-in at that club.

**Stack rulings, already made:** Next.js 15 App Router, TypeScript strict, Tailwind, pnpm, Postgres via Prisma, Auth.js v5 magic link (console transport in dev so no Resend key needed to start), Leaflet with CARTO tiles so there are zero map API keys, haversine in app code, Vitest plus Playwright on iPhone 13 emulation.

## 8. Seed data

44 Boston run clubs, embedded as JSON in the build prompt. Compiled from the Fit Scene Boston guide dated June 2, 2026, plus Yelp, Eventbrite and SweatPals listings.

Fields per club: slug, name, category (national, brand_gym, identity, social, classic), neighborhood, Instagram handle, cost, pace policy, distance note, identity note, social-after note, source URL. Sessions carry weekday, start time, meet spot, coordinates and a precision level.

Roughly two thirds have a seeded weekly schedule. The rest are `check_instagram` or `unknown`.

**Every single fact in it is unverified.** Coordinates are approximate to the stated precision. Times came from an editorial guide, not from the clubs. This is explicitly flagged in the schema and in the UI.

Named clubs include: November Project Boston, Pioneers Run Crew, TrailblazHers, Black Men Run Boston, Heartbreak Hill Running Co., Tracksmith, Midnight Runners, Boston Road Runners, Brighton Bangers, Last Lap Boston, Boston Latina Run Club, Frontrunners Boston, Dashing Whippets Boston, Remnant Brewing Run Club, 88 Run Club, Castle Island Track Club, Davis Square Runners, Portico Point to Pint Runners, and about 25 more.
