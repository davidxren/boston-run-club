## Heylo

Public group pages (`heylo.com/g/<id>`) list upcoming events without login: title, date, time, RSVP count, with per-event links at `heylo.com/event/<id>`. Confirmed live for Pioneers and Boston Road Runners with dated Sept 2026 events. There is no ICS or calendar feed. Heylo offers per-event add-to-calendar only, and a paid embed widget the group owner installs on their own site. Times on the group page appear to be UTC; convert before trusting.

Implication for the product: for the Heylo clubs (Pioneers, Boston Road Runners, Black Men Run, TrailblazHers, Midnight Runners, Boston Latina, LiveFitArmy) set `heyloUrl` and link out. Do not scrape times.
